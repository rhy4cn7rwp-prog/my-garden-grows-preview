# Playtest the twelve neighborhood activities

Open the permanent preview in Safari. From the Clubhouse choose **Go to Town**,
then **Job Board**. All twelve activities are available without dream purchases.
Settings lets you switch between Little Gardener and Growing Gardener without
changing the shared farm or wallet.

- **Hidden Treasures of the Farm:** start when the picture is loaded. Little
  Gardener has five picture clues; tap a clue to hear its name. Growing Gardener
  has ten to twelve written clues (swipe the checklist to see all). Tap objects
  in the scene, pinch or use +/− to zoom, drag to look around, or ask for a Hint.
  Fit shows the whole picture again. There is no deadline.
- **Piece It Together:** select a picture before beginning. Tap a tile next to the
  empty space. Little Gardener has a 3×3 puzzle and a small preview; Growing
  Gardener has 4×4 and can choose a preview. Hint highlights a tile and describes
  its destination. Restart keeps the same scramble and active time.
- **Neighborhood weeding:** drag the soil in either direction to explore the
  overhead bed. Pull golden grassy weeds; healthy feathery carrot tops stay put.
  There are four weeds around each plant. This does not change main Garden
  weeding or its harvest bonus.
- **Pick Tomatoes:** explore ten plants in either direction. Collect all twenty
  ripe tomatoes; green tomatoes stay on the plant. Picking never moves the row.
- **Harvest Stack Sort:** tap a source crate and a destination, or drag the top
  vegetable. Empty and matching crates accept vegetables when there is room.
  Undo and Restart remain available. Growing crates are eight vegetables deep;
  Little crates gradually grow from three to five with completed stack jobs.

Finishing a job pays $1. Beating an existing comparable personal best pays $3
**total**, not $1 plus $3. First finishes and ties pay $1. Each activity rests for
five real minutes after paying. The Leaderboard shows records for the current
learning mode. Pause, background and closed-app time do not count toward records.

The automated mobile checks use Chromium touch emulation, three iPhone-sized
usable viewports and simulated safe areas. Actual iPhone Safari playtesting is
still valuable for pinch gestures, dynamic toolbars and the device's speech/audio
settings. Existing saves remain on this website; no reset or import is required.
